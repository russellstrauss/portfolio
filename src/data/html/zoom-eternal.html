Zoom Eternal is a controller-first, physics-based arena combat game built in Unity that focuses on expressive movement, impactful collisions, and distinct robot play styles. Players select from three unique bots—like the propeller-powered brawler and the hammer-swinging Pecker Wrecker—each with its own control scheme, abilities, and enemy AI behavior. Matches take place in battle arenas filled with environmental hazards that can damage or disrupt players, while power-ups introduce tactical choices by modifying speed, health, and survivability mid-fight. The experience is rounded out with dynamic sound design and a driving soundtrack, creating a high-energy, arcade-style feel that's easy to pick up but rewards skillful play.
