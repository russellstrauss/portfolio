Immersive Art is a browser-based VR studio for sketching, sculpting, and painting in three dimensions using WebXR. Built with Vue and Babylon.js, it places you on a subtle glowing platform under a panoramic starfield dome, then turns your VR controllers into creative tools: lasers that trace light, ribbons and strokes that carve colorful paths through space, floating blocks and bubbles you can spawn, resize, and rearrange, and erasers to quickly reshape your scene. A series of in-world color walls let you pick and sync vibrant hues across all tools, while a custom cursor system, lighting, and menu blocks around you make the interface feel like part of the environment rather than an overlay. The result is an experimental, playful sandbox for interactive 3D drawing—ideal for exploring spatial design, generative sketches, or simply making immersive light sculptures directly inside the browser.
