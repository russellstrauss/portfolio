Algorhythm is an interactive 3D rhythm sequencer that turns world music patterns into a visual playground. A rotating "rhythm wheel" maps beats around a circle, letting you see grooves unfold in real time as you build them—kick, snare, bongos, parade drums, and more—all color‑coded by instrument.

Designed for both curiosity and craft, the app lets you:

  • Load authentic patterns from Africa, the Caribbean, the Americas, and beyond, or start from an empty kit.
  • Shape grooves intuitively by clicking cells on the wheel, adjusting tempo and pattern length with simple controls.
  • Hear and see rhythm at once, making it ideal for teaching, experimentation, and performance sketching.
